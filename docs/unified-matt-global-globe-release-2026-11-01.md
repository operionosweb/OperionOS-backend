## Unified assistant architecture

`MattExperience` is the shared view for main Matt, Contract Workspace Overview,
the workspace assistant tab, authenticated ContractAssistantPanel and floating
Matt. The former floating Operion AI now presents Matt, with expansion into the
same scoped experience. Customer floating Matt is gated by existing contract-read
permission; organization and backend authorization remain authoritative.

`mattSessions` shares a single in-memory conversation for each user/organization
and contract, or each explicitly illustrative flight. Changing context cannot
display another scope's result. Returning to the same scope restores its history.
Identity/organization/permission changes clear retained sessions. No customer
conversation is saved in browser storage. Requests have cancellation and stale
response protection; closing or interrupting a pending interaction aborts it.
Controls have instance-unique accessibility IDs.

The previous Ask Operion workspace's deterministic intelligence engine was
extracted, not replaced by a smaller list of canned answers. Risk, lifecycle,
modeled exposure, obligations, compliance, performance, counterparties, actions,
comparison, document search and missing-data findings remain available, with
source citations, limits, prepared recommendations and follow-up prompts.
Existing grounded contract endpoints remain unchanged. Main Matt selects the
authorized contract and existing document analysis; embedded workspace Matt uses
the explicitly selected analysis version. Neither starts extraction automatically.

The existing relationship engine is preserved as a shared adapter, not another
conversation system. Other demonstration agreements clearly disclose their reused,
non-independent sample evidence. Flight questions never inherit the sample lease.

Evidence is expandable in place. `OPEN FULL REPORT` is an explicit navigation to
the full retained response, findings, evidence, suggestions and limitations;
returning to Matt preserves the conversation. This is not a fabricated notice,
download, signature or automatically approved action.

## Globe implementation and verification

Both public/Demo and customer viewers use the shared bundled geographic Earth
base; detailed satellite/topographic layers are optional overlays, never the only
source of geography. Remote outages retain real coastlines and terrain. Constructor
failure removes Cesium's partially inserted error overlay so the explicit WebGL
fallback does not obstruct fleet selection.

UTC solar lighting is real Cesium sun shading, not a dark CSS background.
The shared imagery lifecycle synchronizes the clock to current UTC every minute
and removes the interval on teardown/style changes. It does not run an unnecessary
continuous simulation clock. Lighting fade distances are explicitly set because
Cesium's defaults wash out day/night shading at the cockpit's overview altitude.
Dynamic atmosphere follows the sun. Equinox, solstice and UTC rotation are tested;
actual central Earth luminance was 162.9 at equinox noon versus 55.8 at midnight.
Night geography remains discernible rather than becoming an unreadable black ball.

All 20 simulated flights are selectable through the global fleet by default.
Airport coordinates, great-circle routes, aircraft heading, along-route motion
and approximate route distance share the same geometry. The routes are:

LHR-SFO, BER-PEK, DXB-SYD, HND-SIN, CDG-JFK, MAD-GRU, JNB-LHR,
SIN-SYD, YYZ-FRA, DOH-HND, BCN-FCO, AMS-MAD, FRA-ARN, LHR-ATH,
NBO-DXB, GRU-LIS, LAX-HND, BKK-MEL, IST-CPT and JFK-MEX.

They span six inhabited continents, with unique flight IDs and fictional
flight-specific clauses, notice obligations, risks, operational exposure and
conditional legal research leads. Diversion/rerouting labels describe a contingency,
not a falsely drawn operational track. Aircraft remain on their stated scheduled
route; no live telemetry, executed agreement or actual diversion is asserted.

Spatial aircraft cards show provenance, route/time facts, unknown ownership and
airframe fields, classified financial exposure, obligations, conditional legal
scope and proposed review actions. On mobile they retain Demo's 2px boundaries,
bounded internal scrolling and unobstructed map controls. Contract inspection
stays in the Globe; closing restores the selected aircraft and keyboard focus.

Illustrative monetary inputs are typed hypothetical arithmetic or unknown, never
confirmed liabilities. No delay alone proves breach or entitlement. Fuel costs
remain unknown rather than borrowing an unrelated scenario credit. A three-hour
question is explicitly a user hypothetical, not a mutation of the flight record.
Legal entries are source-linked research candidates (including conditional
EU/UK passenger-rights and Montreal Convention scope), not applicable-law opinions.

Ask Matt opens the same assistant using only the selected flight's evidence.
Customer Globe handoffs instead use an explicitly associated, organization-scoped
contract from the existing authorized endpoint; demonstration flights cannot
substitute for customer evidence.
